## 2. Institutional Discovery Pitch Framework
1. **The Core Inefficiency**: Treasury desks burn 4+ hours daily manually checking clearing windows and central bank rate spreads across disparate systems.
2. **The Terminal Solution**: One unified, sub-second terminal displaying macro liquidity, FX cross-rates, live payment rail settlement states, and quantitative AI strategy execution.
3. **The Pilot Ask**: A 2-week zero-risk pilot tracking their top 3 high-volume emerging market corridors.
